    Cooked the way my father cooked

    Dera Angeethi started as a roadside coal stove on Lahore's Ferozepur Road in 2009. Today we still grill over real charcoal, marinate for 24 hours and make our masalas in-house each week.

    No shortcuts, no artificial colour. Just good meat, hot coals and people who care how it tastes.
